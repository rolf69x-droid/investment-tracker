Your units and bank purchase prices

Confirmed bank quantities and average purchase prices are saved on this device. Update both after purchases or reinvestments.

Units are stored separately on each device. Bank-style gain/loss = (NAV − bank average purchase price) × units. This matches the bank’s displayed price comparison; it is not a cash-flow or distribution-inclusive total return.

Data source

Historical prices from 15 September to 12 November 2026 are included from Union Investment’s historical-price export. Future prices are collected from public Raiffeisen fund pages. The app displays the actual published NAV date, which may lag behind the calendar date. No investment credentials are required.
